🔍 I am a Research Scientist at Meta in the **Applied AI (AAI)** org, working on
the post-training of Meta's frontier large language models, the
[**Muse**](https://ai.meta.com/) family. My contributions span:

- 🧠 **LLM research**: post-training, data, and evaluation methods that improve
  reasoning, instruction-following, and overall model quality.
- 🎙️ **Speech, audio & music**: my current focus, advancing Muse's
  understanding and generation across the audio domain.
- 💻 **Coding**: expert-level tasks, benchmarks, and training data that
  strengthen the models' software engineering abilities.

Before joining AAI in June 2026, I was on Meta's **Media Foundation Video team
(AI)**, building transformer- and diffusion-based models for video enhancement,
compression, and quality optimization across Meta's Family of Apps (Facebook,
Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger), and collaborating with the **Meta
Superintelligence Lab** on large vision models for video generation and
multimodal understanding.

🎓 I completed my Ph.D. at Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) in 2025,
conducting research at the
[Document and Pattern Recognition Lab (DPRL)](https://www.cs.rit.edu/~dprl/index.html),
under the mentorship of [Dr. Richard Zanibbi](https://www.cs.rit.edu/~rlaz/).
My dissertation,
[*Parsing of Math Formulas and Chemical Diagrams using Graph-Based Representation and Attention Models*](https://repository.rit.edu/theses/12254/),
designed fast, efficient, and interpretable parsers for recognizing math
formulas and chemical diagrams across PDFs, typeset images, and handwritten
strokes, using graph attention in a multi-task learning framework.
Earlier, I was an Applied Scientist Intern at **Amazon (Alexa Speaker
Understanding AI)**, improving speaker identification with semi-supervised
learning.

🧑‍⚖️ I serve as a peer reviewer for *Pattern Recognition* and *IJDAR*, and on
the ICDAR program committee (2023, 2025, 2026), where I was named an IAPR
Outstanding Reviewer in 2025. I am also a Full Member of Sigma Xi. See my
[review service](/review/).

💻 **Ph.D. Projects:**
- **Multimodal Chemical Search**: A multimodal search tool for retrieving chemical reactions, molecular structures, and associated text from scientific literature, linking visual and textual representations of chemical information.
- **ChemScraper**: A molecule diagram parser for extracting molecular graphics from PDFs.
- **MathDeck**: A math-aware search system supporting formulas & text.
