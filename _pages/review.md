I serve as an invited peer reviewer for journals and as a program committee
member for the International Conference on Document Analysis and Recognition
(ICDAR). My reviews focus on math and chemical diagram recognition, document
understanding, graph neural networks, and multimodal / vision-language models.

## Topics Reviewed

Handwritten and printed mathematical expression recognition · molecular image
recognition · graph neural networks for document and diagram understanding ·
document retrieval for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) · vision-language
models for document generation and understanding · evaluation of multimodal
large models on formula recognition · writer identification.
