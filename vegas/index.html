⚠ Key Reminders
🎫
Reserve Red Rock timed entry: required 8 AM–5 PM, Oct–May. $20/vehicle + $2 fee on Recreation.gov. Aim for 9:30–10 AM on Sunday.
🎓
Class Monday at 5:30 PM: leave Vegas by 9 AM so you're home by ~1:30–3 PM even with a slow drive. Pack Sunday night so Monday morning is just checkout. Monday-morning I-15 S is usually lighter than the Sunday-afternoon rush, but check live traffic.
🌙
Pick Sunday evening now: Fremont + Neon Museum, Mystère, or Sphere. Neon Museum night tours and good show seats sell out.
🍜
Thai ordering tip: ask for no fish sauce, oyster sauce, shrimp paste or meat stock. Many "vegetarian" Thai dishes use them by default.
📶
Download offline maps: much of Red Rock has no cell signal.

Across the street · Free
Must-SeeFreeEvening
Fountain shows every 15 min after 8 PM (every 30 min earlier). The Conservatory's Fall 2026 display, "Casting Tranquility", runs through Nov 7. Have a look at the lobby's glass ceiling too.
💡 Best at dusk. Grab a spot along the lake railing.

🏜 Beyond the Strip (car needed)
🏜
Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area
~30–45 min west · $20/vehicle + $2 reservation · Loop open 6 AM–7 PM in October
Must-GoDrive
13-mile one-way scenic drive past red and cream sandstone. Good short stops: Calico I & II viewpoints, the Sandstone Quarry walk, and High Point Overlook. October light is ideal.
⚠ Timed entry required 8 AM–5 PM. No cell signal in much of the park, so download maps first.
Reserve timed entry Fall hours

~10 min drive · Omega Mart ~$45 per person · AREA15 building free
